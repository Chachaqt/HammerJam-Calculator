💡 How the potion math works:

🔨 Builder Potion (10x Speed)

Runs for 1 real hour at 10x speed.

Completes 10 hours of builder progress in 1 hr → Saves 9 hours per potion.

⚗️ Research Potion (24x Speed)

Runs for 1 real hour at 24x speed.

Completes 24 hours (1 full day) of laboratory research in 1 hr → Saves 23 hours! (Laboratory only)

🐾 Pet Potion (24x Speed)

Runs for 1 real hour at 24x speed.

Completes 24 hours of Hero Pet upgrades in 1 hr → Saves 23 hours! (Pet House only)

  • Builder Example: 10h upgrade + 1 Builder Potion finishes in 1 real hour (9h saved)!
  • Lab Example: 3-day (72h) research + 2 Research Potions → 48h done in 2h + 24h normal = finishes in 26 real hours (saves 46 hours)!
